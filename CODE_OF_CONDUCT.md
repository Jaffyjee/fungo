# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The FUNGO community aims to provide a respectful, welcoming, and constructive environment for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and technical issues without personal attacks.
- Share accurate information and distinguish verified facts from opinions.
- Respect privacy. Never publish another person's personal information without permission.
- Do not impersonate project maintainers or misrepresent project status.
- Do not spam, harass, threaten, scam, or encourage malicious activity.
- Do not promise profits, guaranteed returns, listings, rewards, or token outcomes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, hate speech, intimidation, doxxing, threats, deliberate misinformation, scams, and repeated disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub contact or issue-reporting mechanisms. Do not post sensitive personal information, passwords, private keys, or seed phrases in public issues.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and applied consistently.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces, repository contributions, and community interactions represented as official FUNGO spaces.
