# FUNGO Community Guide

## Welcome
FUNGO is a proposed community meme project. Participation should be fun, respectful, transparent, and voluntary. No member should feel pressured to buy, hold, promote, or contribute money.

## Community standards
- Be respectful. No harassment, threats, hate, doxxing, or targeted abuse.
- No impersonation of project contributors, moderators, exchanges, or other communities.
- No spam, referral abuse, phishing, fake giveaways, or unsolicited promotions.
- Do not coordinate wash trading, pump-and-dump activity, spoofing, or deceptive volume.
- Clearly label opinions, jokes, speculation, and unofficial fan content.
- Do not promise profits or present price targets as facts.
- Raise disagreements through constructive discussion and transparent proposals.

## Safety rules
- Never share a seed phrase, private key, recovery phrase, wallet backup, or one-time code.
- Moderators will never ask for wallet credentials or request remote access.
- Verify contract addresses from this repository and confirmed official channels.
- Treat unsolicited DMs, airdrop links, claim pages, and support accounts as suspicious.
- Never sign a transaction you do not understand.
- Fake support staff and cloned websites may use similar names and logos.

## Official channels
No external social accounts or community links are confirmed here yet. Links will be added only after they are created and verified. Do not assume an account is official because it uses the FUNGO name or logo.

## Moderation
Moderators may remove scams, spam, abusive content, and impersonation. Enforcement should be consistent, proportionate, and documented where practical. Report issues through repository GitHub Issues without disclosing sensitive personal information.

## Participation
Contribute documentation, translations, accessibility improvements, design feedback, and constructive proposals. See CONTRIBUTING.md. Holding a token, if one is launched, does not grant guaranteed rewards, governance rights, or financial benefits unless a future verified document explicitly establishes them.
