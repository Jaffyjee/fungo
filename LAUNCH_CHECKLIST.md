# FUNGO Launch Readiness Checklist

**Do not announce a launch as imminent or live until relevant items below are verified.**

## Brand and communications
- [ ] Name and ticker availability checked on target ecosystem
- [ ] Brand assets reviewed for third-party intellectual-property conflicts
- [ ] Website and social accounts controlled by project maintainers
- [ ] Official links cross-verified and listed in README
- [ ] Scam warning and FAQ published
- [ ] Moderation coverage and incident contact established

## Token design and deployment
- [ ] Chain and token standard selected
- [ ] Tokenomics approved and published in TOKENOMICS.md
- [ ] Contract source reviewed and deployment reproducible where possible
- [ ] Privileged functions and authorities disclosed
- [ ] Independent security review completed or status accurately disclosed
- [ ] Deployment transaction and address independently verified
- [ ] Metadata, supply, decimals, and explorer display checked

## Distribution and liquidity
- [ ] Allocation percentages total 100%
- [ ] Wallets and vesting disclosures published
- [ ] Liquidity source and amount documented
- [ ] LP lock / burn claim supported by verifiable evidence
- [ ] Treasury controls and multisig signers disclosed where applicable
- [ ] No undisclosed team or insider allocation

## Legal and operational
- [ ] Relevant jurisdictions and restrictions reviewed by qualified advisers
- [ ] Promotional language reviewed; no guaranteed-return claims
- [ ] Privacy and website terms published if collecting user data
- [ ] Incident response process tested
- [ ] Recovery and access controls documented

## Final publication
- [ ] TOKEN.md completed with verified evidence
- [ ] README.md updated with official links and launch status
- [ ] Change log entry added with date and sources
- [ ] Community announcement matches published facts
- [ ] All TBD fields clearly resolved or explicitly remain TBD
