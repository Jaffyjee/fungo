## Principal risks

1. **Market risk:** Token prices can change rapidly and may fall to zero.
2. **Liquidity risk:** There may be insufficient buyers or pool depth to exit a position at a desired price.
3. **Concentration risk:** A small number of wallets may control a material share of supply. Holder labels and wallet relationships may be incomplete.
4. **Bonding-curve risk:** Trading conditions and prices on a bonding curve can change sharply with buys and sells. Curve completion and graduation are not guaranteed.
5. **Migration risk:** A future migration depends on protocol conditions and successful on-chain execution. Do not assume a PumpSwap pool exists until its address and transaction are verified.
6. **Smart-contract / protocol risk:** Bugs, exploits, upgrades, program failures, or third-party integrations can cause losses.
7. **Wallet and custody risk:** Lost seed phrases, compromised devices, malicious approvals, and phishing can result in irreversible loss.
8. **Information risk:** Social posts, screenshots, token tickers, and unofficial websites can be forged. Verify the mint from controlled project channels and independent explorers.
9. **Regulatory and tax risk:** Laws and tax treatment vary by jurisdiction and may change.
10. **Operational risk:** Project contributors, infrastructure providers, social platforms, or launch platforms may become unavailable or cease support.

## Wallet safety

- Never share a seed phrase, private key, recovery phrase, or wallet backup.
- Never sign a transaction you do not understand.
- Verify the full mint address; do not rely on ticker or token name alone.
- Use only links published by verified project channels.
- Treat unsolicited airdrops, claim pages, support DMs, and giveaway links as suspicious.
- Revoke suspicious token approvals where applicable, using trusted tools.
- Do not send funds to anyone claiming they can guarantee returns, unlock withdrawals, or accelerate graduation.
